# SIDRA Phase 10 Acceptance Contract
- Founder-only callable Functions protect administrative writes.
- Visible words and sentences are controlled through CMS entries.
- Pricing, discounts, commissions, subscriptions, payout thresholds, payout holds, deposits, and cancellation settings are founder-managed.
- Money is stored in paise.
- Finance ledger and audit collections deny direct client writes.
- Administrative changes create audit records.
- SIDRA controls the luxury design boundary; sellers do not receive unrestricted visual control.
