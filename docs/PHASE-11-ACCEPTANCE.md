# SIDRA Phase 11 Acceptance Contract
- Seller growth data is scoped to the authenticated seller Studio.
- Analytics deny direct client writes.
- Coupon codes are normalized and percentage discounts cannot exceed 90 percent.
- Customer segments remain private to their Studio and founder.
- Campaigns can target only a segment owned by the same Studio.
- Campaigns remain drafts until later delivery infrastructure activates sending.
- Seller growth tools cannot override founder commerce controls or SIDRA luxury design boundaries.
